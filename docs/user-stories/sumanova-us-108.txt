=========================================================================================
sumanova-us-108 · Hair that moves — per-lock secondary motion for hair, ponytails & braids
=========================================================================================

USER STORY
  As a SUMANOVA user, I want my avatar's hair to react to every movement — long hair
  swaying when I walk, a ponytail swinging when I dance, braids swinging slowly and heavily,
  fringe bouncing when I turn my head, and a gentle breeze when I'm just standing — so my
  avatar feels alive, while short cuts stay neat and the hair never cuts through my face,
  neck or shoulders.

WHY (what build-103 does today)
  - render/SculptHair.tsx has ONE damped spring for the whole hairstyle. Its offset
    (uniform uSway) is added to every vertex as pow(t, 1.6) · smoothstep(0.05, −0.35, y), so
    the whole lower hair slides sideways as one block: no swing, no rotation, no difference
    between a fringe, side locks, a ponytail or a heavy braid.
  - Only translation of the head is used; head turns, nods and tilts barely move the hair.
  - Nothing stops a moved tip from going into the neck, shoulders or face.
  - The spring allocates new Vector3/Quaternion objects every frame (GC churn on phones).
  - Reduce-motion (Settings → html.reduce-motion / OS setting) is not respected by the hair.
  - The groom stores no per-lock information (vertex colour = shade, t, random; uv =
    across, t), so the shader cannot tell which lock or which group a vertex belongs to.

1) PER-LOCK DATA IN THE GROOM — sculpt3d/groom.ts
   a. StrandMesh.add(): accept two new options and write them per vertex:
        group: number   // which motion group the lock belongs to (see table)
        pivot: V        // the point the lock swings about (its root, or the tie point)
      Store them as new attributes:
        hairPivot (vec3, float32)  — head-local pivot of the lock
        hairGrp   (float32)        — group id; −1 = never moves
   b. Motion groups (assign in emit()/callers, based on where the root is and what it is):
        0 fringe / bangs          (fringe locks, baby hairs of us-107)   light, fast
        1 left side locks          (root x < −0.25·R, below the crown)
        2 right side locks         (root x >  0.25·R)
        3 back locks               (root z < −0.2·rz)
        4 top / crown locks        (everything else on the scalp)       stiffest
        5 ponytail / tail 1        (tail(): pivot = tie point T)
        6 tail 2 / second plait    (twin ponies, double braids, side pony)
        7 single plait / braid     (plait() hanging part: pivot = top of the hanging part)
       −1 static: scalp cap (baseCap), fade/edge shells, buns, cornrows, the band, the
          over-the-head part of French/Dutch plaits, afro volume
   c. Lock stiffness by length: store nothing extra — the shader derives it from
      t (root → tip, vertex colour .g) and the group; short crops (all lock lengths
      < 0.45 head radii) must end up visually still.
   d. GroomOut stays the same shape; the strands geometry simply gains the two attributes.

2) KEEP THE ATTRIBUTES THROUGH WORKER + CACHE — sculpt3d/jobs.ts
   - Add ['hairPivot', 3, 'f'] and ['hairGrp', 1, 'f'] to ATTRS so pack()/unpack() carry
     them through postMessage and Cache Storage (today unknown attributes are dropped).
   - Bump SCULPT_VERSION 80 → 81 (the hair job output changes).
   - Do NOT bump snv-thumbs-v37 unless a still image changes (it must not).

3) THE MOTION SOLVER — new render/hairMotion.ts (pure TypeScript, unit-testable)
   a. export class HairMotion { step(dt, headPose): void; offsets: Float32Array }
      One solver per SculptHair instance. 8 groups, each a 2-segment pendulum
      (mid point + tip point) expressed as small angles (swing about x and z axes) plus
      a little translation, in HEAD-LOCAL space.
   b. Inputs every frame (read in useFrame from the hair group's world matrix):
        - head linear acceleration (from position history)  → inertial push
        - head angular velocity (from quaternion history)    → whip on turns/nods
        - gravity direction in head space                    → hair falls when the head
                                                               tilts / bows (sad, sleeping)
        - optional breeze: low-frequency noise (2 octaves, 0.3–0.7 Hz), amplitude
          0.015 rad, only when the figure is standing still
   c. Per-group constants (tune by eye, keep in one table):
                       freq(Hz)  damping  maxAngle  weight
        fringe   0       2.6       0.35     0.25      0.6
        sides    1,2     1.8       0.30     0.35      1.0
        back     3       1.6       0.30     0.35      1.0
        top      4       2.4       0.45     0.12      0.4
        tail     5,6     1.2       0.18     0.70      1.0
        braid    7       0.9       0.22     0.55      1.0   (heavy: slow, big arc)
      Scale weight by the style's length: weight *= clamp((longest − 0.45) / 1.0, 0, 1)
      for groups 0–4 (crops stay still); tails/braids always move.
   d. Integrate with a FIXED sub-step (1/120 s, max 4 sub-steps per frame) so the motion is
      identical at 30, 60 and 120 fps and never explodes after a long frame (clamp dt to
      0.05 s; reset the state after > 0.25 s, e.g. tab switch).
   e. No allocations inside step(): preallocate every vector/quaternion.
   f. Output: per group a rotation (axis-angle packed as vec3) for the mid and the tip.

4) SHADER — render/SculptHair.tsx (useHairMaterial vertex shader)
   a. Add uniforms: uGrpRot[8] (vec3 axis·angle, mid), uGrpTip[8] (vec3, tip),
      uMotion (0…1 master), uNeck (vec4: capsule centre/radius in head space),
      uShoulder (vec4: ellipsoid), and keep uSway for the baked-hair fallback.
   b. For vertices with hairGrp ≥ 0:
        w   = pow(t, 1.4) (t = color.g, root → tip), weaker near the scalp
        rot = mix(uGrpRot[g], uGrpTip[g], smoothstep(0.4, 1.0, t)) · w · uMotion
        rotate (transformed − hairPivot) about hairPivot by rot (Rodrigues, small angle)
      Rotate the normal and hairT the same way so lighting and the highlight band follow.
   c. Collision (cheap, in the shader, after the rotation):
        - push the vertex out of the neck capsule and the shoulder ellipsoid
          (same shapes as Groomer.keepOffBody: neck cylinder under the jaw, sloping
          shoulders) with a 0.01·R margin;
        - fringe (group 0) and front side locks may never move toward the face: clamp any
          +z motion of vertices in front of the ears when they are below the brow line.
   d. Index the uniform arrays with a float→int cast; keep the shader WebGL1-safe
      (unrolled if-chain if needed for older Android GPUs).
   e. Baked hair (no hairGrp attribute) keeps today's uSway behaviour.

5) DRIVING IT — render/SculptHair.tsx
   - Replace the current spring in useFrame with HairMotion.step(dt, headPose) and copy
     its output into the uniforms (no per-frame allocations: reuse objects in refs).
   - The existing `swing` (how much a style can swing) feeds the per-group weights.
   - animate === false (thumbnails, Avatar Cam head, captures) → uMotion = 0 and state reset.
   - Reduce motion: if the html element has class `reduce-motion` or
     matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)') matches → uMotion = 0 (listen for
     changes). Data saver keeps motion (it's GPU-cheap) but disables the breeze.
   - Pause when the canvas is not visible (document.hidden) — no work in the background.

6) STILL CAPTURES — AvatarStage.tsx
   - capture('full' | 'bust') must render the hair at rest: set a "still" flag that makes
     every SculptHair write uMotion = 0 for the captured frame, then restore it. Profile
     pictures and published body images must not catch the hair mid-swing.

7) HARNESS — e2e/avatar-harness/main.tsx
   - &anim=1 keeps the figures animating (pose from &pose=walking|dancing|waving…).
   - &shake=yaw|nod|tilt plays a scripted head motion (e.g. yaw ±0.6 rad over 0.6 s, then
     hold 2 s) so the lag and settling can be filmed.
   - Add e2e/hair-motion.e2e.cjs: opens the harness for 4 styles, grabs 8 frames
     (every 120 ms) per style into one contact sheet PNG for review.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (attach the contact sheets + numbers)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 A1 Walking & dancing: Long straight, Long wavy, High ponytail, Single braid, Double
    braids, Box braids, Curtains and Wolf cut visibly swing with a lag behind the body;
    the ponytail arcs ≥ 15° in the dance pose; braids swing slower and heavier than loose
    hair.
 A2 Head turn (&shake=yaw): hair tips lag 0.1–0.25 s behind the head and settle within
    1.5 s, with no jitter or endless wobble.
 A3 Head bow / tilt (sad, sleeping, &shake=nod): long hair falls with gravity.
 A4 Short cuts (Buzz cut, Crew cut, Classic short, Textured crop, Mid fade, Caesar cut):
    tips move < 2 mm; buns, cornrows, afros, scalp layer and fades never move.
 A5 No penetration: in every pose and shake, no hair vertex enters the neck, shoulders
    or face; fringes never swing into the eyes.
 A6 Frame-rate independent: the same scripted motion looks the same at 30, 60 and 120 fps
    (sub-stepping); no explosion after a 1 s frame hitch or a tab switch.
 A7 Still images unchanged: thumbnails, starter pictures and studio captures are
    pixel-identical to before (render 3× — no difference), because captures and
    animate=false force the hair to rest.
 A8 Reduce motion (Settings or OS) stops all hair motion immediately; turning it off
    resumes it.
 A9 Performance on a mid-range Android phone: solver ≤ 0.2 ms CPU per avatar per frame,
    no garbage created per frame, studio stays ≥ 55 fps; Map/Profile screens with
    several avatars stay smooth. Hair geometry grows by ≤ 16 bytes per vertex.
 A10 Quality gates: npm run typecheck, npm test, npm run build green; worker + Cache
    Storage round-trip keeps hairPivot/hairGrp (unit test on pack/unpack).
