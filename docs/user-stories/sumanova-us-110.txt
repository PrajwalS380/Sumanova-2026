=========================================================================================
sumanova-us-110 · Total hair control — a hair salon: shape per region, colour placement,
                  and hair accessories
=========================================================================================

USER STORY
  As a SUMANOVA user, I want to fine-tune my chosen hairstyle like a real salon visit —
  trim the top, sides, back and fringe separately, make it neater or messier, colour it
  with roots, lengths and tips, add a money-piece, a peekaboo under-layer or face-framing
  streaks — and finish it with clips, a scrunchie, a headband, flowers or beads, so that
  nobody else has exactly my hair and my avatar beats Bitmoji's fixed styles + one colour.

WHY (what build-103 has today)
  - Shape: one Length slider (scales top/side/back/tail together) and one Volume slider
    (look/hair.ts HairTune.length / volume). You can't keep a long back with a short top,
    shorten only the fringe, or tidy a messy style.
  - Colour: hair + hairTip (root → tip) + hairHi for highlights / streaks / lowlights, and
    7 fixed "paint" effects (render/SculptHair.tsx hairBase()). No mid-length colour, no
    control over WHERE colour goes (money-piece, under-layer, face-framing), no amount.
  - Accessories: none. Hair ties are a hard-coded coral elastic ('band' geometry,
    '#ff6f61'). Story us-109 adds style-specific decorations (paranda, gajra, ribbons,
    beads); there is no general slot to add a clip or flower to ANY style.
  - Colour changes already update on the GPU (uniforms); shape changes regrow the groom in
    the worker — this split must stay.

1) SHAPE PER REGION — look/hair.ts + groom.ts
   a. Extend HairTune (all optional, −1 … 1, normalised in normalizeHairTune):
        top, sides, back, fringe   — length per region
        messy                      — neater (−1) … messier (+1)
        layers                     — blunt (−1) … heavily layered (+1)
      Keep the existing `length` as a master that multiplies all regions (backward
      compatible: a look with only `length` grows exactly as today).
   b. tuneHair(): region factor k = max(0.35, 1 + v · (region long ? 0.35 : 0.5)) applied to
      p.top / p.side / p.back / p.fringeLen respectively (and tail with `back`);
      messy → p.messy = clamp(p.messy + 0.5·v, 0, 1) and p.tips accordingly;
      layers → p.layers = clamp(p.layers + 0.5·v, 0, 1).
   c. hairControls(p): region sliders only when they make sense —
        top: !shaved && afro === 0; sides/back: !shaved && no cornrows/box/locs;
        fringe: p.fringe !== 'none' (or the user turned a fringe on via us-107/existing
        fringe chip); messy/layers: loose styles only (no braids/locs/afro/ties).
   d. The editor sends shape changes with a 150 ms debounce; the worker regrows; the old
      hair stays on screen until the new one is ready (no blank flash). Target: any shape
      edit visible in < 1.5 s on a mid-range phone.

2) COLOUR PLACEMENT — look/config.ts, look/hair.ts, render/SculptHair.tsx
   a. Colours (COLOR_KEYS, all nullable, normalised): keep hair (roots), hairTip (tips),
      hairHi (accent); ADD hairMid (lengths). With hairMid null the gradient is exactly
      today's root → tip.
   b. Add HairTune fields:
        place?: 'none' | 'moneypiece' | 'peekaboo' | 'framing' | 'streak' | 'halo' | 'tips'
        placeAmt?: 0 … 1   (how much / how wide)
        blend?:   0 … 1    (hard line … soft melt between root, mid and tip)
        rootLen?: 0 … 1    (how far the root colour reaches — "shadow root")
      The existing `paint` effects stay (they are presets); `place` is an extra layer on top
      painted with hairHi.
   c. Shader (hairBase in SculptHair.tsx) — 3-stop gradient + placement masks, all on the
      GPU via uniforms (uMid, uPlace, uPlaceAmt, uBlend, uRootLen), using data the groom
      already writes (t root→tip in colour.g, lock random in colour.b, head-local position):
        moneypiece = two front locks either side of the face (front, |x| small, z > 0, above
                     the eyes) in hairHi, width by placeAmt
        peekaboo   = under-layer at the back/sides below the ear line (y < −0.05·R, z < 0.2)
        framing    = locks in front of the ears falling beside the face
        streak     = one bold streak from the part / left front, width by placeAmt
        halo       = a band of colour at mid-length all round
        tips       = dip-dye on the last placeAmt of every lock (hard by default)
      Masks must not depend on lock random alone (or they look patchy): base them on root
      position, which needs the lock ROOT per vertex: add attribute hairRoot (vec3) =
      the point where the lock grows out of the scalp, in groom.ts StrandMesh and to ATTRS
      in jobs.ts. Do NOT reuse us-108's hairPivot for this: hairPivot is the swing point
      (the tie / plait top for tails and braids), so placements would land wrongly on
      ponytails and braids. For locks gathered into a tie, hairRoot is still their scalp
      root (so a money-piece on a ponytail style stays at the front hairline).
   d. Every colour/placement change is uniforms only: no worker job, applied in the same
      frame (< 16 ms).
   e. Studio UI — a "Colour" sub-panel in HairControls.tsx:
        - three swatch buttons Roots / Lengths / Tips (tap one → the existing HAIR colour
          rail edits it; Lengths and Tips have "Same as roots");
        - Blend and Shadow-root sliders;
        - Placement chips (None, Money-piece, Peekaboo, Face-framing, Streak, Halo, Dip-dye)
          + Amount slider + accent colour rail (hairHi);
        - "Natural colours / Fun colours" toggle on the rail (first 19 HAIR entries vs the
          rest) and a "Grey blend" slider (mixes silver into roots + lengths for salt &
          pepper looks).

3) HAIR ACCESSORIES SLOT — `hairAcc`
   a. Data: add optional slots hairAcc1 and hairAcc2 (two accessories can be worn at once)
      to SLOTS / OPTIONAL in look/config.ts, catalogue in a new look/hairAcc.ts with ids
      that never shift, 10 kinds × 10 colourways = 100 items:
        claw clip, snap clips (pair), barrettes (pearl), scrunchie, headband (plain/knotted),
        bow, fresh flower (rose / hibiscus), gajra string, hair pins (gold/silver), braid
        beads & cuffs
      Colours: colour keys hairAcc1 / hairAcc2 (nullable → item default). Reuse the
      decoration builders made for us-109 (gajra, ribbon, beads) — no duplicate code.
   b. Placement uses ANCHORS produced by the groom (new GroomOut.anchors, head-local,
      serialised with the hair job):
        tie (pony/bun base), bunTop, braidEnd[], partFront, sideLeft, sideRight, crownBack,
        overEarL/R, headbandArc (points along the scalp from ear to ear)
      Each accessory kind declares which anchors it accepts (scrunchie → tie; claw clip →
      crownBack or tie; snap clips → sideLeft/sideRight or partFront; headband →
      headbandArc; flower → overEarL/R or bunTop; gajra → bunTop / tie / braidEnd;
      beads → braidEnd[]). If the current style has no fitting anchor the item is shown
      disabled with a hint ("Needs a ponytail or bun").
   c. Rendering: render/HairAccessories.tsx draws sculpted, original pieces (no brands)
      oriented by the anchor's surface normal and comb direction, sitting ON the hair
      surface (offset by the local hair thickness: sample the groom/base cap distance at
      the anchor) and inside the head group so they follow the head and the us-108 motion
      group of their anchor (tie/braid accessories swing with the tail).
   d. With a hat on (hatOn) only accessories outside the hat volume are drawn
      (scrunchie on a low pony, braid beads); others are hidden, not deleted.
   e. Editor: a "Hair accessories" category in the Face & body tab (editor/categories.ts,
      thumb frame 'head', back/side camera for back anchors), grid with thumbnails, colour
      rail, and an anchor picker (L / R / back) where the kind allows more than one.
